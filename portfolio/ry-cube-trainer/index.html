Beginner roadmap

A sensible order to learn

  1. White crossLearn to match each edge with its side center instead of only making a white plus.
  2. First-layer cornersGet comfortable with short triggers such as R U R' U'.
  3. Middle layerPractice inserting an edge to the right and to the left.
  4. Yellow crossRecognize the dot, L and line cases before running the algorithm.
  5. Orient the yellow faceSune is a useful pattern to recognize early.
  6. Last-layer positioningLearn permutation cases gradually instead of trying to memorize everything at once.
